The second axis. Consent is the subscriber's — they subscribe and unsubscribe. Deliverability is the system's: the SendGrid event webhook sandboxes an address the moment it bounces, and only an admin lifts it. A spam complaint is never liftable — the record is kept and the address stays off the list.
