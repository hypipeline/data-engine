Search Coverage

Checks that the entities you expect appear in the registry evidence — a grep over what the search surfaced, not the final recommendation. No analysis LLM runs, so it's fast, cheap and deterministic. Two modes: a URL (fetch + extract + search, one cheap extraction call) or explicit names + jurisdiction (search only, zero LLM). This is exactly what would have caught the herculite, questglobal and Singapore regressions.
